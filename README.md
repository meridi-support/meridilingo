## What is MeridiLingo?

A free, browser-based English expression learning tool that lets you explore **900+ entries across 45 categories** — real-world expressions and AI prompts. From Dead Slang to Modern Slang, Movie & Drama Lines to Travel English, Common Mistakes to Play-by-Play Calls, Japanese Onomatopoeia to AI Prompt Vocabulary for image generation.

New expressions are added regularly — especially Modern Slang and the English you actually hear today.

## ✨ Features

- 🎴 **Card-based UI** — Browse expressions with meaning, example and source
- 💡 **Flip-card trivia** — Tap the 💡 on a card to flip it and read where the word came from
- 🔤 **45 Categories** — Modern Slang, Travel English, Around Town, Common Mistakes, Movie & Drama Lines, Play-by-Play Calls, Pro Wrestling, Anime & Manga, Japanese to English, Wasei-Eigo, AI Prompts and more
- 📅 **Era Filter** — Eras in time order, from Ancient & Heian Period to the 2020s
- 🧩 **Quiz Mode** — Test your knowledge with random quizzes
- 🔀 **Shuffle & Sort** — A-Z, Z-A, oldest / newest first, or random order
- 🎯 **Card Selection** — Click to select, copy multiple expressions
- 🤖 **AI Prompt Cards** — Open a card to see the full ready-to-use prompt and a tip
- 🐾 **Matrix Green Theme** — Dark mode with signature green accent
- 🌐 **100% Browser-based** — No install, no internet required after load, no ads
